Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Overview

Hermes Gmail is a personal-use automation integration operated to send email notifications through a Google account authorized by the operator. It is not offered as a public service.

Google account data and permissions

The integration requests the Gmail send permission (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send) to send operator-configured notification messages. This permission does not authorize reading the inbox. OAuth credentials are used for authorization and are not published on this website.

Data handling

Notification content is generated by the operator's automation workflows and transmitted to Gmail for delivery. The integration may process delivery status and operational error information to support troubleshooting. This website does not request Google account sign-in or collect information through forms.

Third-party services

Google provides the Gmail API used to send messages. GitHub Pages hosts this public informational website. Their respective privacy terms apply to their services.

Access and revocation

The operator can revoke the integration's Google account access from Google Account security settings. Revocation prevents further authorized sending until access is granted again.
